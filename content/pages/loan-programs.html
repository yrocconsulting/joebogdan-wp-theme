Programs Joseph Works With

The Tools Behind the Strategy

Products matter, but only after the plan. These are the programs Joseph most often uses to solve the situations above.

  • Conventional - a range of down-payment options for qualified buyers
  • FHA - more flexible credit and down-payment guidelines
  • VA - for eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses
  • Jumbo - loan amounts above conforming limits
  • Fixed-rate and adjustable-rate - matched to how long you plan to keep the home
  • Non-QM - alternative-documentation programs for borrowers outside standard guidelines
  • Home equity - options to access equity without replacing your first mortgage
  • Down payment assistance - programs for eligible buyers, including first-time homebuyers
  • Bank-statement & 1099 - qualify using business or personal deposits instead of tax returns
  • DSCR - investor loans that qualify on the property’s rental income
  • New construction - financing coordinated with your builder’s timeline

Program availability, rates and terms depend on your full financial picture and are subject to credit approval and change.

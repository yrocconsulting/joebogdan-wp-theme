What Lenders Look At

Not All Land Is Financed the Same Way.

Improved vs. raw land

A lot with road access and utilities in place is generally easier to finance than raw land without them.

Utilities and access

Water or well, sewer or septic, electricity and legal road access all affect whether and how land can be financed.

Survey, flood and restrictions

Surveys, flood zones, zoning, deed restrictions and HOA rules can limit what you can build, so they are checked early.

Your build plans

Buying a lot you plan to build on soon can open different options than land you plan to hold for years.

Texas taxes on land

MUD, PID and county taxes on land, and what they become once a home is built, belong in your budget from day one.

Down payment and terms

Land loans usually require more money down and have different terms than a home mortgage. Joseph will show you what applies to your purchase.

Two Ways to Approach It

Buy the Lot First, or Finance It With the Build?

  • Lot loan now, construction loan later - secure the land you want, then use your equity in it toward the construction loan when you are ready to build
  • Lot and build together - a construction-to-permanent loan can include the lot purchase at the same closing, so there is only one loan
  • Cash for the lot - some buyers pay cash for land and use its value toward the construction loan's down payment

Before the Contract

Verify These Seven Things

  1. Reviewed, not stated. The lender reviewed income documents, bank statements and a credit report.
  2. The right program. The loan program fits the price, the home and how the buyer will use it.
  3. Price and incentives covered. The approval reflects your contract price, and incentives fit the program's contribution limits.
  4. Current home addressed. You know whether the buyer must sell or rent another home, and how that is planned.
  5. Cash to close confirmed. Down payment and remaining costs are documented, including what incentives will not cover.
  6. Timeline fit. The lender has a rate-lock and document-update plan for your expected completion date.
  7. A reachable lender. A named loan officer with an NMLS number who answers your team's questions.

Red Flags

Signs a Contract May Not Close

  • A letter with no lender contact or license number
  • Vague answers about whether documents were reviewed
  • A buyer who needs to sell, while the approval assumes they do not
  • Recent job changes, new debt or large unexplained deposits with no plan
  • Incentives promised beyond the program's contribution limits
  • A lender who is hard to reach before the contract is signed

Questions you can ask the buyer's lender: which documents did you review, which program is this, does the approval cover this price and these incentives, and how will you keep our team updated?

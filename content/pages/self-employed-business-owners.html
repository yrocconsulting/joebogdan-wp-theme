Sound Familiar?

Why Good Businesses Get Bad Mortgage Answers.

“The bank said my income is too low.”

Tax-return income after deductions can understate what you actually earn. Other documentation paths may fit better.

“My income changes year to year.”

Lenders often average income. Timing your application — or choosing a different program — can matter.

“I just switched from W-2 to my own company.”

Recent business owners have options, especially with prior experience in the same field.

“My money is in the business.”

How business funds are used for down payment and reserves needs a plan before you apply.

Options for Business Owners

More Than One Way to Qualify.

Full-documentation loans

Conventional, FHA or jumbo financing using tax returns — often the lowest cost when your returns support it.

Bank-statement loans

Qualify using 12–24 months of business or personal deposits instead of tax returns.

1099 programs

For contractors and commission earners paid on 1099s.

Asset-based loans

Qualify using significant liquid assets rather than monthly income.

Non-traditional programs can carry different rates, down-payment and reserve requirements. Joe will compare them side by side with conventional options.

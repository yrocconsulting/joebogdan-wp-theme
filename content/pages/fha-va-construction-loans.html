Two Government-Backed Paths

Which One Fits You?

FHA one-time close

For buyers who want the more flexible credit and down-payment guidelines of an FHA loan on a home they will live in. FHA loan limits by county and FHA mortgage insurance apply.

VA one-time close

For eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses who want to use their VA home loan benefit to build. There is no monthly mortgage insurance; a VA funding fee applies unless you are exempt.

Conventional one-time close

For buyers who do not need or qualify for FHA or VA, or who are building a home that will not be their primary residence.

How It Works

The Same One-Time Close, With Your Program’s Benefits.

FHA and VA construction-to-permanent loans work like any one-time close: you qualify and close once, the builder is paid in draws as work is inspected, and the loan converts to a permanent 15- or 30-year FHA or VA mortgage when the home is finished. CrossCountry Mortgage offers interest-only payments during the construction phase.

Compare them in detail: FHA vs. VA one-time-close construction loans.

  • Primary residence - FHA and VA construction loans are for a home you will live in
  • Land and build together - buy the lot and fund construction in one loan
  • Eligible builder - your builder must meet the program's requirements; VA requires a builder with a VA builder ID
  • Appraisal from plans - the home is valued as if complete, to program standards
  • Set construction window - typically up to about a year to complete the home

The Questions Buyers Ask Joe Every Week.

“How much can I actually afford?”

Lenders may approve more than you want to spend. Joe shows the payment behind every price point — taxes and insurance included — so you choose your comfort zone.

“Can I buy before I sell?”

Depending on income, equity and reserves, you may be able to carry both homes briefly or use your current equity. Joe maps the options before you write an offer.

It may be less than you think. Conventional, FHA and VA programs each have different down-payment requirements and trade-offs worth understanding.

A fully documented pre-approval and a loan officer who is responsive to the listing agent can help your offer stand out.

Joe won’t pressure you. He will help you compare buying now with waiting, using your real numbers.

Milestones

From Contract to Keys

  1. Contract. Fully reviewed approval, program confirmed, incentives checked against limits, current-home plan in place.
  2. Application and disclosures. The buyer completes the application and receives initial loan disclosures.
  3. Rate-lock decision. Lock now with a longer lock, or float and lock closer to completion, based on the build schedule.
  4. During construction. Monthly completion-date updates shared with the lender; buyer coached to avoid new credit, job changes and large money moves.
  5. Appraisal. Ordered by the lender, from plans or near completion, depending on the program.
  6. Before completion. Updated pay stubs, bank statements and credit as needed; conditions cleared.
  7. Completion. Final inspection or certificate of occupancy, completion inspection if required, final walk-through.
  8. Clear to close. Closing disclosure delivered to the buyer, closing scheduled with title.
  9. Closing and keys. Documents signed, loan funded, keys delivered.

Keep It on Schedule

What Builders Can Do

  • Share realistic completion dates and update them as the build moves
  • Ask the lender to schedule document updates before completion, not after
  • Coordinate the walk-through, inspection and closing date in one shared plan
  • Use a lender who sends proactive updates on every buyer in your pipeline

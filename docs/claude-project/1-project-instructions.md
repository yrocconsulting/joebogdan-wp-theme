## Style
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods or a plain hyphen with spaces.

- Lead with the reader's problem, not the loan product.
- Joseph's differentiator: 30+ years building and running companies (including two decades building medical businesses focused on outpatient diagnostic services) before becoming a loan officer. He brings a business owner's, strategic perspective. Use it naturally, not in every paragraph.
